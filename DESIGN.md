# ACEG Comunicaciones

## Direction

Industrial clarity with an editorial rhythm. White canvas, cobalt blue, graphite text,
a precise lime accent and real communications imagery. Preserve the original ACEG logo.
Distinctive detail: an angled connection line that links the hero, ecosystem and network.

## Reference observations

Motorola Solutions LATAM, reviewed November 2, 2026:
- Two-level navigation separates the brand/search from portfolio navigation.
- Full-width operational photography grounds the opening message.
- Product families and industry entry points are clearly differentiated.
- The ecosystem explains how technologies connect before listing equipment.

ACEG adapts this structure with its own typography, restrained diagonal geometry,
an interactive portfolio explorer and a quieter, shorter navigation.

## Evidence contract

- Surface: institutional B2B website for ACEG, not an ecommerce shop.
- Primary job: understand an operational solution, inspect equipment and contact ACEG or a dealer.
- Hierarchy: ACEG + communications, operational context, ecosystem, equipment, industries, partner network.
- Target viewports: 390x844, 1440x900, 1920x1080.
- No prices, invented customers, invented cases or fabricated dealer addresses.
- Product information and dealer contacts come from the public ACEG/Motorola pages.
- The contact page composes an email for the user; it does not claim backend delivery.
- Search and filters need empty states and a clear reset action.
- Active menus and product selectors must expose keyboard focus and expanded/selected state.
- Image errors preserve aspect ratio and expose descriptive alt text.
- No sign-in, protected area or persistent personal data, so permission state is inapplicable.

## Tokens

- Brand #194C8F; blue #2162BB; graphite #17221F; paper #FFFFFF; surface #F3F5F4.
- Signal #D5F26B is used sparingly on blue or graphite surfaces.
- Typeface: Manrope, with Arial fallback. Display 64px desktop, 39px mobile.
- Content max-width 1320px; desktop gutter 48px; mobile gutter 22px.
- Flat bands, thin rules, small radii, stable image ratios, no decorative floating cards.
- Motion: 180-300ms transitions; reduced-motion preference respected.
